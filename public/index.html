Frequently Asked

Answers, before you ask.

Quick answers about Resumry AI, privacy, pricing, exports, and the tools we offer.

Is Resumry AI free to use?
Yes. Our Free plan includes one AI-generated resume and one cover letter, three free PDF/AI tool uses per day, and basic templates. Upgrade to Pro for unlimited use, premium templates, and the full ATS checker.
How does the ATS Resume Checker work?
Paste any job description, and Resumry maps the role's required skills, keywords, and experience signals against your resume. We score the match out of 100, flag missing keywords, and recommend specific edits to improve fit.
Are resumes from Resumry truly ATS-friendly?
Every template has been validated against major applicant tracking systems (Greenhouse, Lever, Workday, Taleo, iCIMS). We use plain-text-friendly layouts, machine-readable formatting, and no images or graphics that can confuse parsers.
Will my documents be used to train your AI?
No. We do not train any AI model on customer data. Your uploads, drafts, and generated content stay in your account and can be permanently deleted at any time. Files are encrypted in transit and at rest.
What file formats can I export?
PDF and DOCX, optimized for both human readers and ATS parsers. Pro and Business plans also support TXT export and direct emailing.
Can I cancel my subscription anytime?
Yes. There are no contracts. Cancel anytime from your Billing settings and keep access through the end of your current billing cycle.
Does Resumry work in any language?
Resume and cover letter generation currently supports English, Spanish, Portuguese, French, and German. We're adding more — let us know what you need on the Contact page.
How do I import an existing resume?
On the Resume Builder or Cover Letter pages, drop a PDF or DOCX into the upload zone. Resumry extracts your information into editable fields so you can start improving immediately.

Privacy Policy

Your data, your choice.

Last updated · 4 May 2026

Resumry AI is built around the principle that your career documents are private. This policy explains what we collect, why we collect it, and the controls you have. We've tried to write it in plain English — please contact us if anything is unclear.

What we collect

We collect three categories of information: (a) account information you provide when signing up (name, email, plan), (b) the content you create or upload (resumes, cover letters, job descriptions), and (c) usage metadata such as session timestamps, device type, and feature interactions.

How we use it

We use this information to operate Resumry — to generate documents, run ATS analyses, and personalize your dashboard. We use anonymous, aggregated metadata to improve the product. We do not sell your data, and we do not train AI models on your uploaded or generated content.

Where it's stored

Documents are encrypted in transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest (AES-256). They are stored in SOC 2 Type II-compliant infrastructure hosted in the United States. You may request data residency in the EU or Canada on the Business plan.

Your rights

  • Access — download a copy of all your data at any time, from your account settings.
  • Erasure — permanently delete your account and all associated documents.
  • Portability — export resumes and cover letters as PDF, DOCX, or plain text.
  • Correction — edit or correct any of your stored information at any time.
  • Opt-out — disable analytics, marketing emails, or third-party integrations.

Third-party services

We use Stripe for payments, Cloudflare for CDN, and a major cloud provider for compute. Each is subject to its own privacy and security commitments, summarized on our security page.

Contact

Questions, requests, or concerns about your data? Email privacy@resumry.ai. We respond within five business days.
