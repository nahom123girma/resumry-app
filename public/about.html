About Resumry AI

Resumry AI is an AI-powered resume builder, cover letter generator, and ATS resume checker. We help job seekers turn their experience into applications that get past applicant tracking systems and in front of real recruiters.

Try Resumry AI

What Resumry AI does

Applying for jobs is harder than it should be. A strong candidate can be filtered out before a human ever reads their resume, simply because the formatting confuses an applicant tracking system (ATS) or the wording doesn't match the job. Resumry AI exists to close that gap.

The platform brings the whole application into one place: a guided resume builder with a live preview, an AI cover letter generator, a built-in ATS checker that scores your resume against a specific job, and a library of ATS-friendly templates. AI helps rewrite duties into measurable achievements — but you stay in control of every word.

What we believe

Honest tools, not gimmicks

Scores reflect real analysis, exports match what you see on screen, and the product is built to genuinely help you get hired — not to pad numbers.

Your data is yours

Résumé parsing happens in your browser where possible, and your documents are yours to export and take anywhere.

Fast and focused

Resumry AI is designed to get you from blank page to a polished, recruiter-ready resume in minutes, not hours.
